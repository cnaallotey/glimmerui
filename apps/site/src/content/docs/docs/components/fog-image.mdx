Shows an image through frosted glass: blurred, scattered a little by the glass's grain and washed with fog. Hovering wipes a clear patch that follows the pointer, with a faint bright rim where the glass has just been cleared.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `src` | `string` | required | Image URL. Must be same origin or served with CORS headers. See [Images and CORS](/docs/guides/images/). |
| `alt` | `string` | required | Describes the image for screen readers. |
| `frost` | `number` | `1` | How frosted the glass is, from 0 to 1. |
| `tint` | `string` | `"#f4f4f6"` | Color of the fog, as a hex value. |
| `radius` | `number` | `120` | Radius of the clear patch around the pointer, in CSS pixels. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/) and [Vue](/docs/vue/).

## Behavior

- The clear patch follows the pointer; the glass fogs back over behind it.
- On touch screens, a tap clears a patch at the tapped point, and another tap fogs it again.
- Without WebGL, the element shows the plain image as a `background-image`.
