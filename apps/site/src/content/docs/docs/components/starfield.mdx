A flight through deep space: stars come out of the distance, grow and streak past the edges. Hovering shifts the vanishing point toward the pointer and doubles the speed. Use it as a background: children sit above it.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `"#ffffff"` | Star color, as a hex value. |
| `background` | `string` | `"#05040a"` | Sky color, as a hex value. |
| `density` | `number` | `1` | How many stars, as a multiplier. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | Flight speed. Hovering doubles it. `0` holds the stars still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- Stars stretch into short streaks as they pass, longer the closer they are.
- The speed eases up and down with hover, so the jump to warp is never sudden.
- With reduced motion, the stars hold still.
- Without WebGL, the element gets a few CSS stars over the sky color.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
