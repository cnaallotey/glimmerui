A [TiltCard](/docs/components/tilt-card/) with a holographic foil over the image, like a rare trading card. The foil's rainbow bands shift as the card tilts, and a scatter of glints flashes at particular angles. The foil shows faintly at rest and fully on hover.

## Props

`HoloCard` takes every [TiltCard prop](/docs/components/tilt-card/#props), plus:

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `foil` | `number` | `0.8` | Strength of the holographic foil, from 0 to 1. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/) and [Vue](/docs/vue/).

## Behavior

- Like `TiltCard`, the card is drawn at about 70% of the element's height so its edges stay inside the element at full tilt.
- The foil's colors depend on the tilt, so they move with the card rather than on their own.
- Without WebGL, the element shows the image as a `background-image` with the same corner radius, without the foil.
