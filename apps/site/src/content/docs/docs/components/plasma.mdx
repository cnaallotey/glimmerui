Strands of light that wander, flare and fade, each a hot core inside a wide glow. They bend toward the pointer and burn brighter near it.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `colors` | `[string, string, string]` | `["#3b1d8f", "#e0457b", "#ffe9c9"]` | Three hex colors: the outer glow, the strands, and their hot core. |
| `background` | `string` | `"#0a0812"` | Color behind the strands, as a hex value. |
| `scale` | `number` | `1` | How fine the strands are. Higher is busier. |
| `intensity` | `number` | `1` | Brightness. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the strands move. `0` holds them still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- The first color also tints the whole background a little.
- Without WebGL, the element gets two soft CSS gradients of the first two colors over `background`.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
