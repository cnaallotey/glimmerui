The web of bright lines that sunlight makes on the bottom of a pool, shifting as the water moves. The water is deeper toward the bottom of the element and lighter toward the top. Hovering sends rings out from the pointer through the pattern.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `deep` | `string` | `"#06283d"` | Water color at the bottom of the element, as a hex value. |
| `shallow` | `string` | `"#1b8fa6"` | Water color at the top of the element, as a hex value. |
| `light` | `string` | `"#e6fbff"` | Color of the light lines, as a hex value. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the water moves. `0` holds it still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- The light lines are brighter toward the top, where the water is shallower.
- Rings from the pointer fade in on hover and out when the pointer leaves.
- Without WebGL, the element gets a CSS gradient from `deep` to `shallow`.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
