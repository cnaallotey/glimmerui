Shafts of light falling from a point above the element, widening, parting and closing as they drift. Hovering leans the light toward the pointer. Good behind a hero headline or an empty state.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `"#ffbe78"` | Color of the light, as a hex value. |
| `background` | `string` | `"#0c0d14"` | Color behind the light, as a hex value. Dark colors work best. |
| `origin` | `number` | `0.5` | Where the light comes from along the top edge, from 0 (left) to 1 (right). |
| `spread` | `number` | `1` | How wide the rays fan out, from 0.2 to 2. |
| `intensity` | `number` | `1` | Brightness of the light. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the shafts drift. `0` holds them still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- The light swings toward the pointer slowly, over about a second, and back when the pointer leaves.
- A fine grain hides banding in the long gradients.
- Without WebGL, the element gets a CSS radial gradient from `color` to `background`.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
