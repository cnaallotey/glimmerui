A card with a beam of light that runs around its border and glows on the fill beside it. Hovering speeds it up. Good for a card that's working on something, or one you want noticed.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `"#f9b44c"` | Color at the head of the beam, as a hex value. |
| `tail` | `string` | `"#e0457b"` | Color the beam fades through behind its head, as a hex value. |
| `background` | `string` | `"#16151c"` | Card fill color, as a hex value. |
| `radius` | `number` | `16` | Corner radius in CSS pixels. Match it with the element's own `border-radius`. |
| `border` | `number` | `1.5` | Border width in CSS pixels. |
| `length` | `number` | `0.3` | Length of the beam as a share of the way around, from 0.05 to 1. |
| `beams` | `number` | `1` | How many beams share the border, from 1 to 4. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the beams run. At `1` a lap takes six seconds. Negative goes the other way. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- The beam keeps the same pace along the sides and around the corners.
- With reduced motion, the beam stays where it is.
- Without WebGL, the card is a plain box in the `background` color.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
