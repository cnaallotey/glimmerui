Folds an image into mirrored wedges around the center, like a kaleidoscope, turning slowly on its own. On hover, moving the pointer left and right turns it further, and up and down zooms in and out.

## Usage

<Tabs syncKey="framework">
  <TabItem label="React">

  </TabItem>

  </TabItem>

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `src` | `string` | required | Image URL. Must be same origin or served with CORS headers. See [Images and CORS](/docs/guides/images/). |
| `alt` | `string` | required | Describes the pattern for screen readers. The image itself isn't recognizable, so describe what people see. |
| `segments` | `number` | `8` | Number of mirrored wedges around the center, at least 2. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the pattern turns on its own. `0` holds it still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/) and [Vue](/docs/vue/).

## Behavior

- Colorful, detailed images make the richest patterns. The wedges sample the middle of the image.
- On touch screens, a tap turns on the pointer's control from the tapped point, and another tap turns it off.
- With reduced motion, the pattern holds still until the pointer moves.
- Without WebGL, the element shows the plain image as a `background-image`.
