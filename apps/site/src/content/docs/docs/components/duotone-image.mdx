Prints an image in two colors: shadows in one, highlights in the other. On hover, the image's original colors spread out from the pointer until they fill it. Leaving draws the duotone back in.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `src` | `string` | required | Image URL. Must be same origin or served with CORS headers. See [Images and CORS](/docs/guides/images/). |
| `alt` | `string` | required | Describes the image for screen readers. |
| `dark` | `string` | `"#3b1d8f"` | Color for the image's shadows, as a hex value. |
| `light` | `string` | `"#f9b44c"` | Color for the image's highlights, as a hex value. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/) and [Vue](/docs/vue/).

## Behavior

- A dark color with a light one gives the classic poster look. Two similar colors give a quieter tint.
- On touch screens, a tap brings the colors in from the tapped point, and another tap returns the duotone.
- With reduced motion, the colors swap without spreading.
- Without WebGL, the element shows the plain image as a `background-image`.
