A stained-glass window of drifting cells, each tinted from your three colors and a little brighter toward its center, joined by dark leading. Hovering lights the cells around the pointer, as if light were shining through behind it.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `colors` | `[string, string, string]` | `["#3b1d8f", "#e0457b", "#f9b44c"]` | Three hex colors the cells are tinted from. |
| `line` | `string` | `"#0e0d12"` | Color of the leading between cells, as a hex value. |
| `scale` | `number` | `6` | Roughly how many cells fit across the element's height. |
| `speed` | `number` | `1` | How fast the cells drift. `0` holds them still. |

The component also takes any `div` attribute. See [React](/docs/react/), [Vue](/docs/vue/) and [Svelte](/docs/svelte/).

## Behavior

- The leading stays about 2.5 pixels wide at any `scale`.
- Without WebGL, the element gets a CSS conic gradient of the three colors.
- The canvas is hidden from assistive technology. Children stay accessible.
